# **Judging Criteria** 

###### 

## Prototype, not MVP

###### Our judging criteria use the word prototype. An MVP is the smallest product that real users can use and give feedback on over time. A prototype has a different scope: it needs to demonstrate that your core idea works and that someone else can experience it.

###### There is no required feature list, tech stack, or level of technical complexity.

###### 

## Baseline for each criterion:

* ###### **Problem & Validation (20%):**    Show that you talked to real users or customers about the problem. Share who you spoke to and what you learned.

* ###### **Solution & Design (20%):**    Show your design process. Wireframes, sketches, or mockups of the user experience all count.

* ###### **Execution & Prototype (25%):**    Show a working prototype that lets people experience your solution. A Figma click-through, deployed build, or demo video can all work.

* ###### **Viability & GTM (15%):**    Explain your value proposition, how the product makes money or sustains itself, and how you would reach your first users.

* ###### **Communication & Product Thinking (20%):**    Everyone on the team presents, you stay within your time, and you explain the reasoning behind your product decisions.

###### 

## Plan for your pitch

###### Your pitch is ***7 minutes\!***

###### We’ve added 2 extra minutes to each team’s presentation time so you have more room to actually demonstrate your product, rather than rushing through your demo.

###### And because you only have seven minutes, you don’t need to walk [judges](https://productbc.ca/build-a-thon/#judges) through every part of your product. Be strategic about what you choose to show. Your goal is to demonstrate the core value of your solution while giving [judges](https://productbc.ca/build-a-thon/#judges) enough context to evaluate all five criteria.

###### If you use a demo video, decide how long it should run so that your overall pitch stays cohesive and effective. A video is recommended if it helps you demonstrate your product clearly, but it is not required.

###### Ultimately, the question [judges](https://productbc.ca/build-a-thon/#judges) are asking is simple: *Does your prototype demonstrate that your solution works?*

###### If you can cover all five criteria within your seven-minute pitch, you’ve met the *baseline*. From there, it’s about how effectively you tell the story and demonstrate the value of what you built.
